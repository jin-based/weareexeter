Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

WeAreExeter is a free app that shows Phillips Exeter Academy athletics schedules, scores, and livestream links. This policy explains what information the app handles.

The short version: WeAreExeter does not collect personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, and no tracking.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. The app has no sign-up or login, and it never asks for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or location. It contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking software.

What stays on your device

The app saves two things, and only on your device:

  • The teams you star (follow)
  • Your reminder settings: whether reminders are on, and how many minutes before a game they arrive

These are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Notifications

If you turn on game reminders, the app asks for permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled by your device itself. We don't use a push notification service, and we never receive a device token or any other identifier for your device.

Information the app downloads

The app downloads the game schedule from our database, which is hosted by Supabase. The request contains no personal information. As with any internet connection, the server receives your device's IP address so it can send the response, and our hosting provider may keep it for a short time in routine server logs. We don't use it to identify, track, or profile anyone.

Schedules and scores come from the public calendar and results feeds on weareexeter.com, and weather forecasts come from Open-Meteo. Our server retrieves these. Your device does not contact those services through the app, and your location is never used.

Links to other websites

Tapping Watch, a game page, or a recap opens weareexeter.com or a streaming website in your browser. Those websites have their own privacy policies, and we don't control what they collect.

Children

WeAreExeter is meant for the Exeter community, including students. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children under 13.

Selling or sharing data

We don't sell, rent, or share personal information. We don't have any to share.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
