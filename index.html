Common questions

Reminders aren't showing up

Reminders only go out for teams you've starred. Check three things: reminders are turned on in the app's ☰ menu, notifications are allowed for WeAreExeter in your phone's Settings, and the game has a set start time. All-day events don't get reminders.

A game time or score is wrong or missing

The schedule comes from weareexeter.com and refreshes every three hours, so a late change can take a while to appear. For same-day changes and cancellations, check weareexeter.com. If something stays wrong, email us.

The bar at the bottom says the data isn't live

The app couldn't reach the schedule, usually because of a weak connection, so it's showing the copy of the schedule built into the app. Pull down on the list to try again.

The Watch button doesn't open my game

Watch opens the school's streaming page, which lists every streamed game. Pick your game there. Only some games are streamed.

My starred teams disappeared

Starred teams are saved only on your phone, with no account, so deleting and reinstalling the app clears them. Star them again from any game card.
